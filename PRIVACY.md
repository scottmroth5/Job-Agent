# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Job Hunt (Job Agent) is a private job search tool. Its owner runs it for their own search and for people who request access and are approved by hand. You must be 18 or older to use it.

## Signing in

You sign in with Google. The app asks Google only for your email address and that it is verified (the `openid` and `email` scopes). It uses them to recognize your account and to email you about it.

## What the app keeps about you

- **Your account:** your email address, the name and note you gave when you asked for access, when you accepted these terms, and whether your account is pending, active, or paused.
- **Your job search:** the jobs, notes, resumes, files, scores, and documents you add or the app makes for you. Each account sees only its own.
- **Your Claude API key,** if you add one: stored encrypted, used only for your own AI requests, and never shown again, to you, the owner, or anyone else.
- **Usage records:** when you last used the app, when it ran AI requests for you and what they cost, so spending can be shown and capped.
- **Feedback you send:** kept for the owner and emailed to them with your address.

## How it is used

- Only to run the app for you. Data is not sold or used for advertising, and it is shared only with the services listed under Who handles your data.
- Job postings and the material you give the app are sent to Anthropic's Claude API when you use an AI feature.
- The app emails you only about your own account (for example, that it was approved). The owner is emailed when someone asks for access.
- The app's administration screens show each account's email address, name, the note given when asking for access, join date, status, last seen time, setup status, whether a key is saved or the account uses the owner's allowance, last pipeline run, number of jobs, AI spending, and feedback, but not the text of your jobs, notes, or knowledge doc, or your key. As the person who runs the server, the owner has technical access to the database, and looks at account data only to keep the app running, or when you ask.

## Usage totals

The app keeps totals to show how it is used. Once a day, the first time you use the app in a browser, the server adds one to totals of visits by approximate location (city, state, and country) and by device type (phone, tablet, or computer). It works out your location from your IP address on the server, without an outside service. It also counts how people arrive at the app, using a general category: LinkedIn, GitHub, the owner's website, the demo, other, or direct. It does not keep your IP address, browser details, or the address you came from, and these totals are not linked to your account, so they remain if you delete it. Because few people use the app, a city total could still suggest who a visit came from. Only the owner sees these totals.

## The owner's Google access

The owner's own Google sign-in lets the app create its own documents in the owner's Drive (`drive.file`), send the owner's report and account emails (`gmail.send`), and, for the owner's inbox feature, read and label job-related mail in the owner's own Gmail (`gmail.readonly`, `gmail.modify`). Nothing from the owner's mailbox is shown to other accounts. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements.

## Who handles your data

- **Amazon Web Services:** the server in the owner's account that stores your data.
- **Anthropic:** job postings and the material you give the app, when you use an AI feature.
- **Google:** sign-in, encrypted backups in the owner's Google Drive, and account and feedback emails sent through Gmail.
- **Job sites:** when your pipeline runs, the job titles in your search settings go to LinkedIn and Himalayas, and your local search location to LinkedIn, to find postings. The owner's own searches also go to Serper, a Google search results service.

## Your choices

Under Settings you can, at any time:

- **Download your data:** everything that is yours, as one file.
- **Remove your Claude key.**
- **Pause your account,** and resume it later.
- **Delete your account:** it and everything in it are deleted at once, apart from the usage totals above, which are not linked to it. Copies inside the encrypted backups age out within about two months.

You can also revoke the app's Google access at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## The public demo

The demo (fictional sample data, no sign-in) counts how it is used with PostHog, an analytics service hosted in the United States: that the demo was opened, with a general source (LinkedIn, GitHub, the owner's website, another site, or none), which screen was viewed, that a job was opened, which sample feature was started or finished, whether it failed (by a fixed category), and the kind of change it stopped. These counts are used only to understand how the demo is used and improve it. Each count carries only fixed names from that list. Never sent: what you type, job details, names, email addresses, page addresses, or the page you came from. The demo sets no cookies and stores no identifier; a random one lasts only while the page is open. PostHog receives your IP address with each request, as any web service does, may use it to estimate your approximate location, and is set not to store it. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, the demo sends no counts. A content blocker also stops them, and the demo works the same. To object, or with questions: {{supportContact}}. The app itself (signed in) sends nothing to PostHog.
