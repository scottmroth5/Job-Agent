# Tuning the prompts

The prompts are the instructions the AI follows for each task. They work well as they are; tune one when the results are consistently not what you want. Your edits apply only to you.

## Where

**Admin > Prompts** lists them:

- **Full-time scoring** and **Fractional scoring:** how a job is judged and scored from 1 to 10.
- **Resume tweaks:** the changes suggested to your resume for a job.
- **Cover letter:** the body of the letter (the greeting and sign-off are added separately).
- **Meeting prep:** the briefing written before an interview or call.

Open one to see the default text, edit it, and **Preview** it filled in with one of your jobs before you save. Every save is kept, so **Restore** takes you back to any earlier version or the default.

## Placeholders

Words in double braces, such as `{{candidateName}}`, `{{company}}`, or `{{roleTitle}}`, are filled in for each job. Keep the ones the default uses; the editor tells you if one is missing or misspelled. Your About you is always given to the AI alongside the prompt, so you do not need to paste it in.

## Edits that work well

- **Tone of letters:** "Write in a warm, direct voice. Short sentences. No buzzwords such as passionate, synergy, or leverage."
- **Length:** "Keep the letter body to three short paragraphs, under 250 words."
- **What to stress:** "Lead with my experience scaling teams; mention healthcare only when the company is in healthcare."
- **Scoring priorities:** "Score below 5 any role that is mainly people management with no product scope." or "Treat company stage Series B to D as a strong plus."
- **Meeting prep focus:** "Include five questions I should ask, focused on how the team measures success."

## Edits to avoid

- Removing placeholders, or the parts of the scoring prompt that describe the score and its reasons: the app reads those.
- Putting facts about yourself in a prompt. They belong in your knowledge doc, where every task can use them.
- Very long prompts. A few clear sentences of guidance beat a page of rules.
