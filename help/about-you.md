# Writing About you

**About you** (in Settings) is what Job Hunt knows about you. Every score, cover letter, resume tweak, and meeting prep is written from it, so the more specific it is, the better they get. Think of it as the briefing you would give a recruiter who is going to represent you.

## The quickest start: Build a draft

In **Settings > About you**, choose **Build a draft** and share what you already have:

- **Your resume,** saved as a PDF (in Word: File, Save as, PDF).
- **Your LinkedIn profile,** saved as a PDF: on your profile, choose **More**, then **Save to PDF**.
- **Anything else useful,** pasted as text: your LinkedIn About section, a job ad you would love, performance review highlights, notes on what you want next.

It writes a draft About you and a starting search for you to read, correct, and save. Nothing is saved until you do, and your files are not kept. Fill in anything marked **[add: …]** and answer the questions it lists; those answers make the biggest difference.

## What to include

1. **Summary:** your level, field, and years, and what you are known for.
2. **Experience highlights:** role by role, with results and numbers (team size, budget, growth, savings, scale).
3. **Strengths,** with proof.
4. **What you want next:** roles, level, industries, company stage, remote or on site, pay.
5. **Not a fit:** what to steer away from. This sharpens scoring as much as anything.
6. **Stories:** two or three accomplishments you would tell in an interview.
7. **Other:** education, certifications, languages.

Write plainly, in the first person. Facts beat adjectives: "grew the platform team from 6 to 40 engineers" tells the AI far more than "strong leader".

## Template

```
## Summary
[Your level and field] with [N] years in [industries]. Known for [two or three things].

## Experience highlights
**[Title], [Company] ([years])**
- [Result with a number]
- [Result with a number]

## Strengths
- [Strength]: [proof]

## What I want next
[Roles and level]. [Industries]. [Company stage and size]. [Remote, hybrid, or on site, and where]. [Pay, if you want it used].

## Not a fit
[Roles, industries, or situations to avoid]

## Stories
- **[Short title]:** [situation, what you did, result]

## Other
[Education, certifications, languages]
```

## Example 1: an engineering leader

```
## Summary
Engineering leader with 15 years in B2B software, the last 7 running teams of 30 to 120 engineers. Known for scaling platforms through growth and for building hiring and on-call practices that last.

## Experience highlights
**VP of Engineering, Example Health Co (2021 to now)**
- Grew engineering from 35 to 110 across four product lines; kept attrition under 8%.
- Led the move from a monolith to services; deploys went from weekly to 40 a day.
**Director of Engineering, Sample Analytics (2016 to 2021)**
- Built the data platform behind a product line that reached $20M ARR.

## Strengths
- Scaling teams: hired and onboarded 80+ engineers with a structured interview loop.
- Reliability: cut major incidents by 70% with on-call and review practices.

## What I want next
VP or SVP of Engineering at a Series B to D software company, 50 to 300 engineers. Healthcare or fintech preferred. Remote or hybrid near Anytown, ST.

## Not a fit
Pure IT or infrastructure-only roles; agencies; roles without a product focus.

## Stories
- **Platform rescue:** inherited a platform with weekly outages; rebuilt on-call and testing; outages fell from 12 a quarter to 2 in nine months.
```

## Example 2: a fractional finance leader

```
## Summary
CFO and finance leader with 20 years across SaaS and consumer brands, now working as a fractional CFO for two or three companies at a time.

## Experience highlights
**Fractional CFO, independent (2022 to now)**
- Built the first operating model and board pack for three seed to Series A startups.
- Led a $12M Series A process from model to close.
**CFO, Example Consumer Co (2015 to 2022)**
- Took revenue from $15M to $90M; ran the finance, legal, and HR teams.

## What I want next
Fractional CFO engagements, 10 to 20 hours a week, at seed to Series B companies. $200 to $300 an hour. Fully remote.

## Not a fit
Full-time roles; bookkeeping-only engagements.
```

These examples are made up: write your own facts.
