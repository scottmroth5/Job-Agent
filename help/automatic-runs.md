# Automatic runs

**Settings > Automatic runs** decides whether your pipeline runs on its own, when, and what it may spend on AI. Turn it off and the app runs only when you press a button.

## On or off

- **Run automatically on the times below** is the main switch. It saves as soon as you click it.
- **Off means manual runs only.** Nothing starts by itself and no AI is used until you press **Run pipeline** on Home (or **Check inbox**). Home and the Inbox show a **Manual runs only** badge so you can see it at a glance.
- Taking a break from your search? Turn it off. Your jobs, letters, and settings stay as they are, and you can turn it back on at any time.

## When it runs

- **Add a time** and tick the days for each one, such as Monday and Thursday at 6:00. You can have several times, such as weekdays at 7:00 and Saturday at 9:00. Click **Save times** when you are done.
- Times are in the time zone shown under the section.
- Only one pipeline runs at a time across the app. If yours comes due while another is running, it waits and starts when that one finishes.
- If the app was down when a run was due, it runs when the app comes back, as long as that was within the last 12 hours.

## What a scheduled run does with AI

A scheduled pipeline always finds new jobs, which costs nothing. You choose whether it also:

- **Scores new jobs:** reads each new posting against your knowledge doc and gives it a score.
- **Writes cover letters and resume tweaks** for the jobs that scored high enough.

Untick either one to keep a scheduled run from spending on it. **Run pipeline** on Home always does every step. See *AI and costs* for what each step costs.

## The inbox (the owner only)

The owner can also have the inbox checked after each scheduled pipeline run, at its own times, or both.
