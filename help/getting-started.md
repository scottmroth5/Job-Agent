# Getting started

Job Hunt finds job postings for you, scores how well each fits, writes tailored cover letters and resume tweaks, and keeps track of every application.

## Set up (once)

Open **Settings** from the Home page:

1. **AI.** Add your Anthropic API key (from console.anthropic.com), unless the owner covers your AI. Scoring, letters, and meeting prep need it.
2. **About you.** Paste your resume and add notes: what you have done, what you are good at, what you want next, and anything to avoid. Everything the AI writes about you comes from here. At least 500 characters.
3. **Your search.** The job titles to search for, words a relevant title contains, and the places you can work in person (remote jobs are always included). Save it.

## Find jobs

- **Run pipeline** on the Home page searches the job sources, scores what it finds, writes materials for the strongest matches, and archives stale ones. It takes a few minutes; you can watch it on the Activity tab.
- Turn on **Run my pipeline on the app's schedule** in Settings to have it run by itself.
- **Add a job** adds one you found yourself, from its link or by pasting the description.

## Work through them

Open **Jobs**. The default view shows what needs your action. Open a job to read its score and why, its letter, your notes, and files. Change its status as you go (applied, interviewing, and so on); the **Funnel** shows how your search is going.
