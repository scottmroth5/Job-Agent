# Jobs, statuses, and materials

## The Jobs screen

- **Stage** says where a job is in the process: *Discovered* (found, not yet promising), *Pipeline* (worth acting on), *Archived* (done with).
- **Status** says what is happening: new, applied, interviewing, offer, passed, rejected, and so on.
- The default filter, **Needs action**, shows pipeline jobs waiting on you. Search by title, company, or place; **Advanced filters** add track, score, and dates.

## A job's panel

- **Score** (1 to 10) and the reasons for it, from your About you and the posting. Without the posting's full text a score is capped at 5; paste the description to score it properly.
- **Cover letter** and **resume tweaks**, written for jobs that score well. **Regenerate** makes a fresh version; nothing is overwritten.
- **Prepare for a meeting** writes a briefing for an interview or call: who you are meeting, what to ask, and what to stress. It can research the company on the web first.
- **Notes** (dated, kept separately) and **files** (PDFs and images open in the browser).
- **Status** changes are kept as history, which the Funnel uses.

## Tracks

Jobs are **full-time** or **fractional** (part-time leadership roles). Fractional jobs also show fit, pay, and hours, and an annual estimate when the pay is known.
