# Jobs, statuses, and materials

## The Jobs screen

- **Stage** says where a job is in the process: *Discovered* (found, not yet promising), *Pipeline* (worth acting on), *Archived* (done with).
- **Status** says what is happening: new, applied, interviewing, offer, passed, rejected, and so on.
- The default filter, **Needs action**, shows pipeline jobs waiting on you. Search by title, company, or place; **Advanced filters** add track, score, and dates.

## A job's panel

- **Score** (1 to 10) and the reasons for it, from your knowledge doc and the posting. Without the posting's full text a score is capped at 5; paste the description to score it properly.
- **Cover letter** and **resume tweaks**, written for jobs that score well. **Regenerate** makes a fresh version; nothing is overwritten.
- **Working on a letter:** **Revise with Claude** takes your feedback ("lead with the platform rebuild", "make it warmer") and returns a revised draft with a note on what changed; keep going as many rounds as you like, or edit the draft yourself. Nothing is saved until **Use this version**. **Use my own version** saves a letter you wrote, pasted or from a Word, PDF, or text file. **Versions** lists every version; any one can be viewed, downloaded, or made current again. **Download** gives the letter as Word or PDF, with your header (**Settings > Cover letter header**: name, email, phone, location, links, and sign-off name; the email and links are clickable), the date, greeting, and sign-off. Revisions use your AI (about 2 cents each) and only the facts in your knowledge doc and resume.
- **Prepare for a meeting** writes a briefing for an interview or call: who you are meeting, what to ask, and what to stress. It can research the company on the web first.
- **Notes** (dated, kept separately) and **files** (PDFs and images open in the browser).
- **Status** changes are kept as history, which the Funnel uses.

## Following up on applications

Most applications go through a careers site or a job board, with nobody to write back to. Following up through that system does nothing; reaching a person does.

- **Worth a follow-up:** an application with no reply shows on Home and in the job's **Next step** from 7 days after you applied, until it is stale. Archived jobs never show.
- **Find someone to contact** (in Next step) searches the web on your AI for the likely hiring manager (who the role reports to) and a recruiter, and drafts a LinkedIn connection note and a short email from your knowledge doc. It costs about 10 to 30 cents and is saved under the job's **Notes**. It never contacts anyone: check each person on LinkedIn before you write. Send one note, at most one short nudge a week later, then let it go. A referral from someone you know at the company beats any of this, so look for one first.
- **Stale:** after **Settings > Follow-ups** days with no reply (45 unless you change it), an application is stale. After that long the answer is almost always no. Home shows one item for all of them: **Review** lists them, and **Close all as no response** closes them with a note on each. **Close: no response** in a job's Next step closes just that one.
- **No response** is a closed status; closed applications still count in the Funnel.

## Tracks

Jobs are **full-time** or **fractional** (part-time leadership roles). Fractional jobs also show fit, pay, and hours, and an annual estimate when the pay is known.
