# AI and what it costs

## What uses AI

Scoring jobs, writing cover letters and resume tweaks, meeting prep, and the answers on this Help page. Everything else (finding jobs, tracking, the funnel) works without it.

## Who pays

- **Your own key:** add your Anthropic API key in Settings. Anthropic bills you directly; set a monthly limit in your Anthropic console if you want one. The app checks the key with Anthropic, stores it encrypted, and never shows it again, to you or anyone else.
- **The owner's key:** the owner may cover your AI up to a monthly allowance. Settings shows how much you have used. When it runs out, AI pauses until the next month or until the owner raises it.

Without a key or an allowance, AI features tell you to add a key; everything else keeps working.

## Rough costs

A typical pipeline run that scores a few dozen jobs and writes a couple of letters costs well under a dollar. A meeting prep with web research is one of the larger items, about one to two dollars. **Admin > Spending** shows this month's spend by feature.
